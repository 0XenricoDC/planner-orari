<objective>
Fix Bug C from RESEARCH.md: When an employee is the only worker assigned to their department on a given day (`isSoloDept = true`), Phase 2.5 locks the shift end to `baseEnd` (8h fixed). Across 5 full work days this accumulates 5 × 8h + 3.5h half-day = 43.5h vs the 40h contract, with no way for Phase 3c to reduce it.

Purpose: The business reason for the original isSoloDept end-lock is to guarantee store-closing coverage when a single worker is on duty. But locking end on EVERY day prevents the dynamic hours-targeting that Phase 2.5 relies on. The fix relaxes the lock to fire ONLY when needed for coverage: (a) the last full work day (when the algorithm cannot self-correct later), or (b) when hoursLeft ≥ avgShiftH (a full shift is still needed anyway, so locking changes nothing).

Output: Modified `shift-planner.html` with conditional isSoloDept end-lock in Phase 2.5.
</objective>
